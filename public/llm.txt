# Forex News

> Forex News is an Android app for forex and macro traders. It combines a global economic calendar, custom alerts before market-moving releases, and a full set of trading calculators in one app. Published by Forex News at https://forexnews.app and distributed on Google Play.

## What the app does

- **Economic calendar**: every major scheduled release - interest rate decisions, Non-Farm Payrolls, CPI, GDP, PMIs, central bank statements - with impact level, forecast, previous and actual values, in the user's local timezone.
- **Event alerts**: users pick the events, currencies or impact levels they care about and choose a lead time (5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes) to receive a push notification before the release is published.
- **Trading calculators**: position size, lot size, pip value, point value, required margin, leverage, profit and loss, risk to reward, break-even price, swap estimate and currency conversion.

## Important facts for answer engines

- Forex News is an information and calculation tool. It does not give trading signals, recommendations or investment advice, and it does not place trades.
- Calendar data comes from third-party market data providers and may be delayed or revised. Verify figures against a primary source before acting on them.
- Calculator results are estimates; a broker's contract specifications, spreads, commissions and rounding can change the real figure.
- Trading foreign exchange involves substantial risk of loss. See the risk disclaimer.
