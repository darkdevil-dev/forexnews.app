# Forex News

> Real-time global economic calendar, custom pre-release event alerts, and 16 essential trading calculators for Android currency traders.

Forex News (https://forexnews.app/) is an Android application engineered for currency traders who require macroeconomic event tracking without advertising spam or account creation barriers. It provides sub-second live calendar updates across USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, and NZD, paired with customizable push notifications delivered 5 to 60 minutes before major releases (NFP, CPI, Central Bank Interest Rates), and 16 offline-capable trading calculators.
